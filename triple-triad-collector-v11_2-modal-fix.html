How to Play

  1. Select Mode (Classic/Draft), Difficulty, and Board. Start a match.
  2. Turns alternate. Place a card on an empty tile.
  3. Same: if two or more adjacent opposing sides exactly match your placed sides, those cards flip.
  4. Plus: if two or more sums (your side + opposing side) are equal, those cards flip.
  5. If neither Same nor Plus triggers, basic rule compares touching numbers and flips lower adjacents.
  6. Combo: any cards flipped by Same/Plus then perform one basic capture pass to their neighbors.
  7. Type Advantage: attacking side gains bonuses/penalties based on Pokémon type effectiveness.
  8. Elemental tiles: cards on an elemental tile get +1 if matching type, otherwise −1 (min 1, max 10).
  9. Best‑Card Loot: on win, you capture the AI’s strongest card.
